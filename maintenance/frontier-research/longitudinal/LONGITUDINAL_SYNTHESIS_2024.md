## Year question
What changed across 2024 in what must accompany an evaluation or research claim before it can be interpreted without collapsing score, evidence, benchmark, evaluator and provenance?

## Stage progression
| Stage | Durable movement |
|---|---|
| A / Q1 | score is placed inside an evidence envelope |

## Full-year synthesis
The 2024 evidence model evolves toward:

```text
claim / research question
-> task/domain scope
-> benchmark object + vintage
-> dataset/reference construction
-> contamination/freshness state
-> evaluator/judge + rubric/prompt/config
-> public/private data boundary
-> model/provider/output provenance
-> raw result
-> uncertainty/conflict/correction state
-> bounded interpretation
```

This is not a universal schema and does not make a result true. It prevents distinct evidence axes from collapsing into one score.

## Cross-year durable boundaries
1. score != truth;
2. benchmark family != fixed measurement instance;
3. updated benchmark != rewritten old result;
4. evaluator agreement != context-free authority;
5. reference construction quality != zero dataset error;
6. freshness != difficulty;
7. private/held-out evaluation != independently replayable evidence;
8. claim transfer != acceptance;
9. provenance != truth.

## What remains unresolved
No universal normalization exists across changing benchmarks, task scopes or judge protocols. No Stage establishes contamination-free certification, universal judge authority or cross-benchmark score equivalence.
