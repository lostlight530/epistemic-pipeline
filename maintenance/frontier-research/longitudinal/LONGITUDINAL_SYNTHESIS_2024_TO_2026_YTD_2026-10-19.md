## Persisting mechanism, negative space and competing explanations
claim indexed != claim true; evidence linked != evidence sufficient; heuristic score != probability; runtime-policy pass != truth; claim transfer != acceptance; provenance != truth.
The defensible connection is a **research question progression**: identifying an object → binding version and environment → relating actions/transformations and source claims → testing which inferences an evidence trail cannot support. Nothing in the cross-year comparison proves that one selected upstream project caused another's innovation, that a user actually adopted the revision, or that a versioned release fixed a defect in this repository.

Sources are not independent cross-validations merely because they occur in different quarters or projects.
