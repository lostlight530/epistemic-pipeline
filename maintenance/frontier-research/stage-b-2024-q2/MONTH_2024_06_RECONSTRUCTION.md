## Research interpretation

June shows two responses to benchmark aging

MMLU-Pro rebuilds a familiar benchmark family into a harder, more reasoning-focused and experimentally less prompt-sensitive form

LiveBench makes temporal renewal part of benchmark design itself

The comparison object therefore changes from a model score to a structured observation containing:

- model revision
- benchmark family/version/vintage
- task distribution
- prompt/judge/scoring method
- contamination state
- execution date

## Boundary

No model ranking is carried into this repository

The research object is evaluation semantics, not which model scored highest
