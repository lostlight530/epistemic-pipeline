# E1 — HLE Difficulty, Scope and Calibration

## Research question
What changes when a benchmark is intentionally constructed near the expert frontier rather than merely refreshed?

## Evidence
Humanity's Last Exam (arXiv:2501.14249, 2025-01-24) introduces 3,000 multi-domain, multimodal closed-ended questions written by subject-matter experts. The paper frames the benchmark as a response to saturation of popular benchmarks and reports low accuracy and calibration for then-current models.

## Analysis
HLE makes difficulty and calibration explicit parts of the measurement contract, but difficulty does not erase scope:

```text
high difficulty
+ expert-authored questions
+ broad subjects
!= universal intelligence
!= open-ended research competence
```

The benchmark remains closed-ended and automatically gradable by design. That is a measurement choice, not a defect.

## Provenance boundary
The paper/source family is authoritative for benchmark construction and reported evaluations. This Stage does not independently rerun the dataset or certify every item.
