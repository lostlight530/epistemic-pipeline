## Rationale
Stage D established evaluation as a bounded measurement contract: benchmark vintage, task scope, reference construction, evaluator/judge protocol and split identity all matter. Stage E asks what changes when the evaluated object itself moves from static difficult questions toward economic software tasks and underspecified problems that require information acquisition.

## Research questions
1. What does HLE add when a benchmark is intentionally difficult, broad and expert-authored?
2. What does SWE-Lancer add when task identity includes real freelance artifacts, payout value and end-to-end grading?
3. What does QuestBench add when a task is intentionally underspecified and success depends on asking for missing information?
