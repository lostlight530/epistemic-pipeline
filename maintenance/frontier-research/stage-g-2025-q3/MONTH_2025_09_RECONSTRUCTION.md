# September 2025 Reconstruction — Memory Under Partial Observability

## Monthly event
UltraHorizon appeared in late September 2025 as a long-horizon, partially observable agent benchmark emphasizing sustained planning, memory, and large tool-call trajectories.

## Historical interpretation
September stretches the evidence problem over time.

```text
what was observable
-> what was retained
-> what plan followed
-> which action changed state
-> what new evidence arrived
-> how memory/plan changed
```

Once trajectories become long, current repository/state snapshots cannot substitute for task-time state. A later-complete transcript also cannot prove what was available to the agent at an earlier decision point.

## Month boundary
No UltraHorizon rollout or model inference was executed. Long context and tool-call counts are workload descriptors, not competence scores.
