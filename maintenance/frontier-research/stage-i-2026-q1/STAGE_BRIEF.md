## Research design
**Question:** Q1: handoff history configuration, human approvals and immutable tool metadata distinguish recorded trajectory from authorized and recoverable state.

**Repository lens:** claim evidence, agent trace, approval, evaluator, environment and uncertainty. Carry from preceding quarter: Stage H 2025 native handoff

**Eligibility:** exact official upstream GitHub releases published within quarter, selected for distinct versioned technical questions. Exclude later releases and secondary reposts; hold claims of local execution, broad adoption, scientific validity or independent conformance. One tagged release = one event object, even when the changelog has many bullets.

**Discovery actually performed:** 2026-10-19 GitHub REST upstream `/repos/OWNER/REPO/releases?per_page=100&page=N` listing inspection followed by exact `/repos/OWNER/REPO/releases/tags/TAG` retrieval for the selected versions. No systematic scholar search, full archival survey or independent replay. Sources: openai/openai-agents-python.

**Planned/actual Parts:** I1_HANDOFF_HISTORY.md (S1 / O1); I2_HITL_APPROVAL.md (S2 / O2); I3_IMMUTABLE_TOOL_META.md (S3 / O3). Chart by date, release, publisher-origin, observable upstream claim, analytic implication, counter-reading and validation. Review performed by the same research producer, not an independent expert.

**Non-claims:** claim linked != claim true; score != truth; approval != appropriate authorization. Outputs are month reconstructions, research Parts, source/object register, evidence chart, synthesis, review, handoff and provenance statement.
